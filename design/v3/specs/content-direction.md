# V3 copy and imagery direction

## Voice

Plain, confident and helpful. Speak to an adult building an everyday routine. Prefer product identity, clear options and useful information to exaggerated optimization language. Hero headlines can be strong while body copy stays short and specific. Use the supplied copy dictionary for layout labels; it is design copy pending merchant review, not approval of health claims.

## Facts and promises

Current label and approved product content govern ingredients, directions, warnings and benefits. Do not derive an ingredient list from a generated package image. Do not transfer one product's warning to another or omit it because a concept screenshot lacks it. No fabricated clinical proof, certification, doctor recommendation, customer quote, satisfaction percentage, sales count or review seed. Do not infer safety for a medical condition or audience from the everyday-wellness design.

Prices, availability and offers are live commerce content. The dated product snapshot is for research comparison and examples only. A hypothetical subscription tile must remain hidden until a real offer exists. A third-party original container such as Myos MD or N1O1 keeps its true branding.

## Editorial model

The example protein article is a layout sample and contains neutral draft copy. Future articles need their own substantive research, an actual author/reviewer if shown, publication metadata and distinct imagery. Do not copy archived campaign layouts or present generated people as named doctors or customers. Text-first article previews are preferable to repeating the same image across six posts.

## Photography

Everyday adults, natural light, real tactile textures and believable routines. Inclusive ages and appearances, no extreme bodybuilding or dramatic clinical imagery. White, subtle light blue, linen, water and warm stone support the product identity. No empty sterile laboratory used as proof of testing. No reused homepage photo as a contact/wholesale filler image. Decorative photography has no implied factual endorsement.

## Final copy review

Check actual product name/format, exact label, relevant restrictions, offer eligibility, operational promises, page destination and image interpretation before publishing. Questions that depend on a business decision stay in DECISIONS.md rather than becoming confident-sounding invented copy.
